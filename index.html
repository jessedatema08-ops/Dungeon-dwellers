Rules & Systems
D&D 5e 2024 RevisedCharacter data is authoritative. The AI verifies actions, equipment, spells, resources, conditions, targeting, and legal mechanics before resolution.
Player-Owned RollsPlayers enact attacks, damage, saves, checks, initiative, death saves, concentration, healing, rerolls, and other player-facing dice through the app.
ReactionsValid triggers open a 1-hour player reaction window. The AI never chooses a reaction for a player.
Scene TurnsOutside combat, asynchronous scene actions are gathered and resolved by the AI DM while questions about known information remain free.
Private InformationPlayer-specific map visibility, knowledge, hidden enemies, traps, secret doors, and split-party information are scoped through Supabase access rules and visibility records.
